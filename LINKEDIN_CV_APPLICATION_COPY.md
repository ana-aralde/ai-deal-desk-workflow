## Interview version

I wanted to build something directly relevant to Deal Desk rather than a generic AI demo. I created a Streamlit workflow that can ingest a fictional RFP or procurement questionnaire, extract requirements, classify and route them to functions such as Security, Privacy, Legal, or Finance, retrieve approved canonical sources, and use AI to create requirement-level drafts. I then added a response matrix and a human release gate so unsupported claims are surfaced instead of invented and AI recommendations never silently replace the deterministic baseline. The project helped me translate operational controls, source governance, escalation logic, and human approval into a working AI-assisted workflow.
